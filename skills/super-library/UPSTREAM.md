# Provenance of the narrative revision

The 2026-10-09 revision is an independent synthesis, not a copied or adapted
third-party skill. Super Library remains the owner of retrieval and AI-paper
composition; no second narrative-writing skill is introduced.

The reviewed inputs are published primary papers already in the corpus.
[The paragraph study](../../library/studies/experimental_narrative_2026-10.json)
records exact PDF URLs, SHA-256 identities, section/page locators, review date,
independent summaries, and limits for seven sources. PDF checksums serve as
artifact identities; no third-party executable commit was imported. The external
papers retain their own rights. Neither their full text nor copied sentences
are included in this skill or relicensed as corpus data. Original examples are
marked as patterns; structural summaries are marked `paraphrased_synthesis`.

Runtime capabilities are unchanged: selective local reads, local lookup/lint,
and primary-source verification when needed. No model endpoint, credentials,
background job, external publication, or installation is added. Generated
references are rebuilt from canonical repository data, not downloaded prose.

The new activation fixtures include a retained-table analysis request and a
non-writing numerical-analysis exclusion. Native schema, budget, mirror, CLI,
and retrieval tests verify runtime artifacts. Editorial examples and regex
passes do not establish effectiveness; the paired benchmark requires independent
ratings and pinned generation conditions before any measured improvement claim.
