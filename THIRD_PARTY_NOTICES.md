# Third-party notices

Super Library stores bibliographic facts and links to primary papers so writers
can locate and verify evidence. It does not redistribute paper PDFs, abstracts,
tables, figures, or substantial passages.

Paper titles, author names, venue names, identifiers, and URLs identify external
works. Copyright and trademark rights in those works remain with their respective
authors, publishers, and rights holders. Following a link does not grant reuse
permission.

Short attested collocations are indexed only to document ordinary research usage.
Their locators point back to independent primary sources; they are not presented
as quotations or as substitutes for reading those sources. All longer example
sentences and structural templates in this repository were independently written.
